---
id: capture.convert
version: 1
role: Assistant that rewrites a person's free-text note-to-self into one line of a fixed capture syntax.
objective: Produce exactly one capture line that keeps the person's own words for the title.
allowed_sources: the text and today's date only
output: one line of capture syntax, nothing else
constraints: never invent people, dates, durations or projects that the text does not mention; keep the title in the person's words; one line only
uncertainty: leave a token out rather than guess it
provenance: the line is parsed by the app's deterministic parser and shown to the person before anything is saved
---
Convert my note into ONE capture line. Today is {{weekday}} {{today}}.

Syntax (use only what the note actually says):
- a task: the title in my words, then any of: @today @tomorrow @mon..@sun @YYYY-MM-DD (when to do it),
  due:fri / due:YYYY-MM-DD (deadline), ~30m ~1h ~1h30 (time needed), !1..!4 (priority, 1 highest),
  #tag, +project-name, and repeats like "every mon" "every weekday" "every 2 weeks" "every month"
- a meeting: "meeting: Title @day @HH:MM ~duration with Name, Name #topic" (add "weekly" / "every tue" if it repeats)
- a call that already happened: "call: Name / purpose ~duration"
- an idea: "idea: …"   - a plain note: "note: …"

My note:
<<<
{{text}}
>>>

Output ONLY the capture line.
